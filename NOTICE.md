## Standards referenced

WESO refers to, but does not reproduce, the following. Designation codes in the examples are used to illustrate how the schemes project onto WESO; codes deeper than the published breakdown are marked as hypothetical.

- IEC 81346 and the RDS family (RDS-PP, RDS-PS); the VGB/BDEW guideline *RDS for WTG*, version 2.0 (2024)
- IEC 61400-25
- ISO 14224
- ISO 15926, ISO 10303-239 (PLCS) — discussed as prior art only

Example data (turbines, serial numbers, manufacturer "ACME") is fictitious.
